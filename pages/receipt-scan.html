Scan a receipt

Upload a photo of a bill. OCR reads the merchant, date and total, and you confirm everything before it is saved.

PNG, JPG or WEBP, up to 5 MB

Nothing is saved automatically.

You will review and edit the extracted details on the next screen.

For best results

  1. Lay the receipt flat in good light.
  2. Fill the frame with the receipt; avoid shadows.
  3. Make sure the total line is sharp and readable.
  4. Check the detected amount before saving.
